# Philosophy

In the world of AI assisted development, SaQle wants you to develop with the speed of a vibe coder
while keeping the discipline and standards of a traditional software engineer.

## Convention and configuration

The framework relies heavily on both convention and configuration to get your idea working as fast as possible. 

The conventions applicable will be pointed out alongside other recommended practices to get the best out of this framework.

## Reusable components and resources

SaQle is modular and component based. This ensures that a developer never has to do the same thing twice even across projects. 

Modules and components are self contained and easily transferrable from project to project. You basically write once, use everywhere. in a fast paced customer facing environment, this is a valuable feature of the framework,
